Google OAuth verification: what to submit (Pelex)

1. Website and domain
   - Homepage: https://pelex.tech
   - Privacy policy: https://pelex.tech/privacy.html
   - Terms of service: https://pelex.tech/terms.html
   - Verify pelex.tech in Google Search Console (DNS TXT record in Cloudflare, set to DNS only).
   - Both pages must be live and match what the app does before you submit.

2. OAuth consent screen
   - App name: Pelex
   - Support email: support@pelex.tech
   - Developer contact: hello@pelex.tech
   - Authorised domain: pelex.tech

3. Scopes: request only what each feature needs. Justify each one in one or two sentences.
   - gmail.send (sending an email the user has approved)
   - gmail.readonly (only if the feature reads mail the user chose to search)
   - calendar.readonly or calendar.events (only for meeting suggestions and bookings the user approves)
   - sheets / drive.file (only for files the user creates or picks through Pelex)

4. Demo video (unlisted YouTube), shows:
   - Sign-in, and the consent screen with the scopes listed
   - The user connecting Google, and choosing the feature
   - The approval step before any email is sent
   - Disconnecting Google from Settings

5. Limited Use statement (already in the privacy policy, section 5): keep it exactly as written.

6. Before you submit
   - Do not request scopes for features that are not switched on yet; add them when they are built.
   - Keep the Google features in Testing mode until verification is approved (up to 100 test users).
   - Make sure every claim in the privacy policy is true of the live app on the day you submit.
