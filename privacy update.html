1. Who we are

Pelex (formerly Uncle Pelex AI) is an AI assistant built and run by PelexSkillHub, based in Nigeria ("Pelex", "we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use our website, chat interface, Telegram bots and, where enabled, our WhatsApp channel and business chat widget (the "Service"), why we collect it, and the choices you have.

By using the Service you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

2. What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google, GitHub or email, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from that provider. We never see your Google or GitHub password. Passwords you set with us are never stored in plain text.
  • Your chats. When you are signed in, we save your messages and the assistant's replies so your chat history and Projects appear again when you return. Chats you have without signing in are not saved to an account.
  • Files, images and voice. Pictures, documents and voice recordings you send are processed to produce a reply.
  • Business details. If you set up a Business bot, the brand name, tone, website address and information you give us for the bot to use with your customers.
  • IP address. We use your IP address only to detect your approximate country so we can show prices in the right currency. We do not use it for anything else, and we do not use it to track your precise location.
  • Basic technical data. Standard browser logs needed to keep the Service working and secure.

3. How we use it

  • To answer your messages and run features such as voice, image understanding and web search.
  • To save your chats, greet you by name and remember context between sessions.
  • To show the right currency, process payments and keep your plan active.
  • To carry out tasks you ask for through tools you have connected.
  • To keep the Service secure and prevent abuse, including illegal activity (see our Terms).

We do not sell, rent or monetize your personal data, and we do not use your private chats to advertise to you or to anyone else.

4. How Pelex remembers: two kinds of memory

Pelex keeps two kinds of memory. They are stored separately and treated differently on purpose.

Left: the Personal bot. Right: the Business bot. Bottom: the Gmail assistant that both can use.

1. Normal memory

Everyday conversation is saved so Pelex can recall context and work with you over the long term, without making you repeat yourself. You can switch chat saving off, delete your chats, or download your data in Settings, Privacy.

2. Sensitive memory (Personal bot only)

Only the Personal bot has a sensitive memory. It is where information such as a saved transaction PIN or payment details would live, and only if you choose to save it. It is stored separately from your normal memory and is encrypted. Pelex asks twice before saving anything sensitive:

1. Pelex asks. "Do you want me to save this?"

2. If you say yes, Pelex asks again. "Are you sure you want me to save it?"

3. Only then does it proceed.

If you say no at either step, nothing is saved or used.

Before Pelex acts on a payment for you, it asks permission, asks you to confirm a second time, and then sends a one-time verification code so we know it is really you authorizing it. If any step is declined or the code is wrong, nothing happens. Some payment actions are not available yet.

Business bots never handle sensitive information

The Business bot has normal memory only. It captures leads and does the work the business has set it up for, such as project management and data entry. It does not store or process PINs, card details or payments. Anything about money, a PIN or a transaction is handed to the business owner, who deals with the customer directly.

5. Who can access your data

  • Other users: never. Each signed-in user can only reach their own data.
  • Staff, partners and front-end contributors: no one in these roles can see user data.
  • Sensitive memory: only Pelex's founder, who built the backend, can reach it, and only to fix a fault. It is not read for any routine purpose. If another developer joins, they receive limited access that excludes sensitive memory entirely.
  • Normal memory: the founder, and any authorized developer later on, may look at it only to debug a problem or give support you have asked for.
  • Law and safety: we may disclose information only where the law requires it or to protect people from serious harm.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use your private chats for advertising.

6. Google data and connected accounts

Signing in with Google gives the Service only your basic profile (name, email, picture). If you later activate the Personal assistant and connect Gmail, Calendar, Sheets or other Google services, the Service uses that access only to carry out the tasks you ask for, and requests only the permissions those features need. You can disconnect at any time from Pelex or from your Google account settings.

Pelex's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Other tools you connect, such as Trello or Asana, work the same way: Pelex acts only within the access you grant and only to do what you ask. Some tools may run on a connection owned by Pelex and used only for the specific action you request.

7. Email assistant (Gmail)

If you connect Gmail, Pelex's shared Gmail assistant can help in both the Personal assistant and Business bots. It only works on accounts you connect.

  • Several accounts, each with a switch. You can connect more than one email account, and turn the assistant off for any account at any time. When it is off, Pelex stops reading that account.
  • What it does. Reads and filters your mail, labels it (for example unread mail, or by project into labels you already have), drafts replies, and learns how you write so replies sound like you. You can ask it by the name you gave your bot, from the website, Telegram or WhatsApp.
  • It asks first. Before it creates a new label, or acts on any new suggestion or idea of its own, Pelex proposes it and waits for your approval.
  • Sensitive content is off limits. The Gmail assistant does not read or save PINs or payment details, even if they appear in an email.
  • No people reading your mail. Pelex staff do not read your Gmail content, except with your clear consent for a specific support issue, where needed for security or to stop abuse, or where the law requires it.
  • Google data limits. We use Gmail data only to provide the features you use. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train general AI models.

8. Services that help us run Pelex

Your data is handled by these providers only as needed to deliver the Service: Supabase (accounts and saved chats), Netlify (website hosting), Cloudflare (traffic security), Render and n8n (the assistant backend), OpenRouter (AI language processing), Groq (backup AI language processing and image reading), ElevenLabs (spoken replies and voice transcription), Serper (web search), our email delivery provider (confirmation codes, welcome messages and support replies from hello@pelex.tech and support@pelex.tech), Paystack (payments, when paid plans are available) and Telegram or WhatsApp if you use those channels. We do not sell your data to any of them or to anyone else.

9. How we protect your data

  • Each signed-in user can only access their own saved chats; Supabase row-level security is used to keep accounts separate.
  • Connections are encrypted in transit.
  • Cloudflare helps protect the Service from malicious traffic.
  • Our security screening flags requests that appear to involve illegal activity, and the assistant will not respond to them.
  • Sensitive memory is stored separately and encrypted, and sensitive actions need an extra one-time verification code.
  • Access to systems and stored credentials is limited to what is needed to run the Service. See Who can access your data.

Before release, Pelex was tested for about 60 days by a community of around 40 people who used it day to day and reported problems, as part of our screening process. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not share passwords or card numbers in chat.

10. How long we keep data

  • Account details and saved chats are kept while your account is active.
  • Images and videos that are generated or downloaded through our servers are automatically deleted within 2 days.
  • You can delete your chats or your account at any time in Settings, Privacy, or by emailing us. We delete or anonymize your data within a reasonable time, except records we must keep by law (for example payment records).

11. Who we share data with

Only with the service providers listed above, with accounts you have connected and asked us to act on, and with authorities where the law requires it or to protect people from harm. We do not sell personal data.

12. Your choices and rights

  • Ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, or for it to be corrected or deleted.
  • Switch chat saving or personalised replies off in Settings, Privacy.
  • Decline to have sensitive information saved, with no effect on your use of Pelex.
  • Disconnect any connected account at any time.
  • Use the free chat without signing in.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights under local law. Email us and we will help.

13. Children

The Service is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will remove it.
