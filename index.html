How your privacy is handled

Pelex drafts for you to review and never sends on its own. Attachments and images are not read. Voice input uses your browser's speech service, which may send audio to your device maker. Sign-in tokens are kept on the server, never in your browser.

FAQ

What is the Pelex Pitch Agent?

You tell it what you do and share your resume and portfolio. It finds a few suitable companies, writes a message in your tone and shows it to you first. It only sends after you approve.

What is Pelex and how does it work?

Pelex is an AI agent you talk to by text or voice. Ask it to plan, write, search the web, explain your files and write code. It answers in the chat, and you can save long replies as a PDF or Word file.

What should I use Pelex for?

Planning your week, drafting messages and documents, learning something new, researching with web search, and writing code. The Personal and Business bots work with your own accounts.

How much does it cost?

Chat is free with a daily limit and needs no login. Premium and the bots are paid, and every plan starts with a 2-day free trial. A card is taken at the start, and you can cancel any time during the trial. See the plans above.

Do I need an account?

No. You can chat without one. Signing in saves your chats and library across your devices.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. In Settings you can download your data, delete all your chats, or delete your account.
