Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what Pelex collects, why, where it is kept, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it. Pelex is an AI assistant and business record platform operated from Nigeria by Emmanuel Ephraim Uwem (“Pelex”, “we”, “us”). Questions and requests: hello@pelex.tech. Support: support@pelex.tech.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the features you use: chat, business records, analysis, uploads, scheduled reports and payments.
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent abuse, and apply usage limits.
  • To send service emails you asked for or that the service needs (sign-up confirmation, password help, receipts).
  • To improve reliability. We do not use your business records or chats to train public AI models.

3. Where data is processed and stored

Pelex runs on these providers. Each handles data only to provide its part of the service:

  • Supabase: the database, sign-in, and file storage. Each business is kept separate, and access is limited to its members by row-level security rules.
  • Cloudflare: hosting of the website and the security layer in front of the chat service. It also checks the human-verification step (Turnstile).
  • Render: runs the Pelex server that handles chat, tools and scheduled tasks.
  • AI model providers: OpenRouter, Groq, Anthropic (Claude), Cloudflare Workers AI, Mistral, SambaNova and Google Gemini. When you send a message, the text (and any picture you attach) is sent to the provider that answers it. Pelex removes sensitive details where it can before sending. Provider terms apply to what they receive.
  • Paystack and Flutterwave: payment processing. They receive your payment details directly.
  • Email delivery provider: sends service emails.

Some providers store data outside Nigeria. Where that happens, we rely on their contractual safeguards.

5. Google sign-in and Google Workspace data

Google sign-in is not Gmail access. If you choose “Continue with Google”, Pelex receives your basic Google profile information (such as your name, email address and profile picture) through Supabase Auth so you can sign in. This sign-in does not give Pelex permission to read your Gmail, Calendar, Drive or Sheets data.

Current connector status. Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Google Sheets account connectors are not currently enabled in Pelex. The Gmail Bot currently helps you draft email text and can open a compose window in Gmail. You review and send that email yourself. Pelex does not currently read your Gmail inbox, messages, attachments or labels, and does not send messages through the Gmail API. It does not currently access your Calendar, Drive or Sheets content through Google APIs.

If a Google Workspace connector is introduced in the future, Pelex will explain the feature and request the specific permissions it needs on a separate Google consent screen before accessing that data. You will be able to decline or disconnect the connector. This policy will be updated to describe the actual data accessed, how it is used, where it is processed, how long it is retained, and how to disconnect or request deletion before the connector is made available.

  • Pelex does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
  • Pelex does not use Google Workspace user data to train AI models.
  • When and if a Google API connector is enabled, Pelex’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Any future email-sending connector will require your explicit approval before a message is sent.

6. Sharing

We share information only with the providers listed in section 3, with people you invite to a business you own, with authorities when the law requires, and to protect people’s safety. We do not sell personal data.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and copy: you can see your business records and chats in the app, and ask for a copy of your data.
  • Correct: you can edit your profile and business records.
  • Delete: you can delete chats, uploads and business records, and delete your account. Deleted uploads can be restored for one year.
  • Withdraw: you can turn off chat saving, change keep-times, and stop using optional features at any time. Google sign-in is an authentication method; it does not connect a Google Workspace account.
  • Complain: you may contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To use a right, write to hello@pelex.tech. We respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, row-level security so that each business sees only its own data, an activity record for business changes, and separate keys for each service. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

Pelex is not for children under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update the date at the top when this policy changes, and for important changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
