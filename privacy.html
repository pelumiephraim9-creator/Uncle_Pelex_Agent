1. Who we are

Pelex is an AI assistant built by PelexSkillHub, based in Nigeria. This policy covers the Pelex website and app, the chat, voice and image features, the Business Agent, the Pitch Agent, the Gmail Bot, scheduled tasks, and meeting notes (together, "the Service"). It explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

By using the Service you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

2. What we collect

  • Account details. Name, email, sign-in method (email, Google or GitHub), and onboarding answers you choose to give: date of birth, a category that describes you, your interests, and a short about-you note.
  • Chats and messages. Your messages and Pelex replies, saved so you can return to them, if chat saving is on. You can switch saving off or delete chats at any time.
  • Files, images and voice. Images and documents you upload, and voice audio when you use voice features. Generated images and videos are deleted automatically 2 days after creation.
  • Pitch Agent profile. Your field, years of experience, work modes, location, the countries you are approved to work in, your tone, your portfolio link, and a resume file if you upload one. The Pitch Agent also keeps proposals, approvals and the progress figures it shows you.
  • Business Agent records. Your business name, tone, description, website, limits you set for the bot, revenue entries you record, meetings, customer messages sent to your inbox, and your replies.
  • Gmail data (only for accounts you connect and switch on). Message details needed to show your inbox summaries, drafts, labels you approve, and sign-in tokens. Each connected account has its own on/off switch.
  • Meeting notes. The meeting link you paste, and, when you start notes, the audio or transcript needed to make your chosen output (PDF, voice or digital notes).
  • Technical data. Approximate country (from your connection) so prices show in the right currency, device and browser details, and standard server logs kept for security and fault checking.

3. How we use it

  • To answer you, run the features you ask for, and keep your work where you left it.
  • To schedule and run tasks you set, and to show the results in the app.
  • To search public websites for jobs and clients, read public company information, and build proposals for your approval (Pitch Agent).
  • To show your business revenue, meetings, and inbox, and to let you reply to customers (Business Agent).
  • To draft emails and summaries for the Gmail accounts you switch on (Gmail Bot).
  • To show prices in naira in Nigeria and in dollars elsewhere, and to process plan payments.
  • To keep the Service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell, rent or monetise your personal data, and we do not use your private chats, Gmail content or business records to advertise to you or to anyone else.

4. How Pelex remembers things

Pelex keeps different kinds of memory, and each is used only for its purpose:

  • Chat memory. Earlier messages in a chat, so replies make sense. Turn saving off in Settings, or delete chats at any time.
  • Your preferences. Tone, reply length, and interests you set, used to personalise replies if personalisation is on.
  • Business records. Revenue, meetings and inbox items, kept for the Business Agent only.
  • Gmail. Only for accounts you have switched on. Turning an account off stops Pelex working on it.

What Pelex never handles

No Pelex agent is designed to read, store or act on PINs, passwords, card numbers, bank details or payment instructions. If such details appear in an email, a chat or a customer message, the agent is built to ignore them and not to repeat them. Payments are handled by our payment provider, not by the agents.

5. Who can access your data

  • You. Your data is visible to your signed-in account only. Database access rules keep each user's rows separate.
  • Our team. Only the founder and named technical contributors, and only to support you, fix faults, or respond to a legal request. Access is logged where possible.
  • Admin inbox. Customer messages sent to a business go to that business owner's inbox. Pelex staff do not read them except when the owner asks for support.
  • Service providers listed in section 8, only as needed to run the Service.

6. Permissions and two-step approval

No Pelex agent is allowed to take a sensitive action on its own. This applies to the General chat, the Business Agent, the Pitch Agent, the Gmail Bot, scheduled tasks, and any Google or other third-party app you have connected.

What counts as a sensitive action

  • Sending, replying to, forwarding or deleting an email or message.
  • Archiving, moving, labelling or changing settings in an email account or other connected app.
  • Reading or exporting data from Gmail, Calendar, Drive or another connected app beyond what you asked for.
  • Creating, changing or cancelling meetings or calendar events, or sharing a file or link.
  • Changing business records, prices, bookings, or replies sent to your customers.
  • Publishing or posting anything, starting a meeting recording or transcript, and connecting a new account or app.
  • Anything involving payments.

The two steps

  • Step 1: review. The agent shows exactly what it will do: the account, recipients, subject, content, link or file, and when. You approve, edit, or reject it.
  • Step 2: confirm. Before the action runs, the agent asks you to confirm once more. Nothing runs until both steps are complete.
  • An approval covers only the action shown. It expires if not used within a short time, and a changed action needs fresh approval.
  • Silence, a previous approval, or a scheduled task never counts as approval for a new sensitive action. A scheduled task that needs a sensitive action pauses until you approve it.

Read-only tasks you request in chat, such as summarising one email you choose, run after your request. Bulk reading, exporting, or reading beyond the task is treated as a sensitive action and needs both steps.

We keep a record of approved actions so you can review what was done and when.

7. Google user data

When you sign in with Google, we receive your basic profile (name, email and picture). If you connect Gmail or Calendar later, the Service asks for the specific permissions each feature needs, and only uses them for those features.

Pelex's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising. We do not sell it. We do not let humans read your Gmail content, except where you ask us to for support, or where the law requires it. Gmail content is sent to our AI providers only to deliver the feature you asked for, under contracts that prohibit them from using it to train their models.

You can disconnect any Google account at any time in Settings, or from your Google account settings. Disconnecting removes Pelex's access and stored sign-in tokens.

8. Gmail Bot

  • Several accounts, each with a switch. Turn an account on or off at any time. When it is off, Pelex stops reading or acting on it.
  • Drafts and sends with your approval. Pelex drafts emails and shows them to you. Nothing is sent until you approve it in two steps (see section 6).
  • Labels and summaries. Pelex can suggest a label or a summary. It asks before it creates or changes anything.
  • Sensitive content is left alone. The Gmail Bot is built not to act on PINs, passwords or payment details it sees in an email.
  • Flip-card view. Your recent emails appear as summary cards. They are only shown to you.

9. Pitch Agent (jobs and clients)

  • What it reads. Public company websites, public job pages and public company information (such as published funding or revenue, news and hiring activity). It does not log into private accounts, bypass access controls, or collect personal data about other people beyond what a public job posting shows.
  • Estimates. Company size is estimated from public sources and labelled with its source. If it cannot be verified, the Service says so.
  • Direct links. It prefers the company's own application page, and marks a link as verified only after the page loads.
  • Your approval. It builds a proposal and shows it to you. It sends only after you approve in two steps (see section 6). Approved emails go to the Gmail Bot, which sends from the account you chose.
  • One person, one shortlist. Your shortlist is built for you alone, and is not shared with other users.

10. Business Agent and meeting notes

  • Revenue and meetings. The figures and meetings you enter are used for your own charts and reports.
  • Customer messages. Messages from customers who chat with your business bot are kept in your inbox so you can respond. Replies, price changes and bookings are made only after your two-step approval (see section 6).
  • Meeting notes. You choose the output (PDF, voice or digital notes). Before recording or transcribing a meeting, you must tell the participants and, where the law requires it, get their consent. Recordings and notes stay in your account until you delete them.

11. Services that help us run Pelex

Your data is processed by these providers, only as needed to deliver the Service:

  • Supabase (accounts, database and file storage). Netlify (website hosting and functions). Cloudflare (traffic protection and our agent gateway). Render (our agent backend).
  • AI model providers (for chat, images, vision and summaries), including Google Gemini and other providers the Service uses.
  • Voice providers (speech-to-text and text-to-speech, including ElevenLabs), and the speech service built into your browser or device.
  • Google (sign-in and the Google features you connect). Resend (account emails, when enabled). Paystack (plan payments, when enabled).

We do not sell your data to any of them. Telegram and WhatsApp are not part of the Service.

12. How long we keep data

  • Account details and saved chats: while your account is active, or until you delete them.
  • Generated images and videos: automatically deleted 2 days after creation. Save anything you want to keep.
  • Gmail sign-in tokens: until you disconnect the account. Gmail summaries: until you delete them or disconnect.
  • Meeting recordings and notes: until you delete them.
  • Server logs: kept for a limited period for security and fault checking.
  • You can delete your data or your account from Settings, or by emailing us.

13. How we protect your data

  • Each user's data is separated by database row-level security.
  • Connections are encrypted in transit. Sign-in tokens and provider keys are stored on the server, never in your browser.
  • Sensitive memory and connected accounts are protected with additional access checks.
  • No system is perfectly secure. Please do not share passwords or card numbers in chat.

14. Your choices and rights

  • Ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted.
  • Turn chat saving, personalisation or any Gmail account on or off in Settings.
  • Disconnect a connected account at any time.
  • Object to or restrict some uses of your data, and withdraw consent where we rely on it.

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have these rights. Email hello@pelex.tech and we will respond.

15. International transfers

Some providers process data outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we choose providers that offer appropriate safeguards and tell you about it in this policy.

16. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will remove it.

17. Changes and contact

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app. Questions or requests: hello@pelex.tech.
