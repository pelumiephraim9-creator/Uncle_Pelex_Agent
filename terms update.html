2. What Pelex is

Pelex is an AI assistant available on the web, on Telegram and, where enabled, through WhatsApp and an embeddable chat widget for businesses. It can chat, work with files, images and voice, and use tools you connect. Some features are marked "Coming soon" and are not yet available.
